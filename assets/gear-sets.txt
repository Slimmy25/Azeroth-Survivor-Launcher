Set bonuses, entered by hand from the game's own armory (0.121.0): the set box on an armor piece's card.
@four   rarity | text | stat=value, ...    the 4-piece bonus, the same for every class (4 armor pieces of the set at 1+
        stars). The stats are those the Gear Planner can add; a bonus with a condition is text only.
@eight  class | rarity | text               the 8-piece bonus (all 8 armor pieces at 3+ stars; empowered at 5 stars on all
        eight: half again as strong). Each class's own: a class not entered yet shows "not entered yet".
@stars  rarity | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5          what each star adds to the piece's own bonuses, in percent of its 0-star
        numbers. "top": the 5th star lifts the piece to 95% of the same slot's 5-star tier 3 (Legendary) piece.
        Known from the patch notes: 5 stars make tier 3 60% stronger, and the 5th star of a lower set lifts it to 95%
        of 5-star tier 3. Stars 1 to 4 are ESTIMATES (an even 12% a star) until the game's "Next star adds" is read.
Lines starting with # and everything above the first === line are ignored.
===
