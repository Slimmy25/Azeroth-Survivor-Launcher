Gear bonuses, entered by hand from the game's own item cards (the Armory's Collection: click a piece). The game works
these numbers out as it runs, so they are not in its files like the rest of assets/items.txt.
One piece per line:   class | rarity | slot | health | bonus=value, bonus=value, ...
rarity: Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, PvP, Legendary.   slot: Head, Shoulder, Chest, Waist, Legs, Feet, Wrist, Hands, Main Hand (a hunter's weapon: Ranged), Off Hand (a rogue's second dagger, a shaman's shield)
bonus (as the card says it; percent bonuses as their percent):
  damage, cooldown, health% (max health), crit, critdmg, armor (damage reduction), regen (health per second),
  speed, area, pickup, xp, gold, luck, echo (chance to cast twice), lifesteal, killheal, targets, thorns, buff
Lines starting with # and everything above the first === line are ignored.
