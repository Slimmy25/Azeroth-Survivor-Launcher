# What it is
A free helper for Azeroth Survivors, the game made by Aethus. Slimjim made it for the community. Everything in it is optional.

# What it does
- Installs the game: one click downloads it from the official hosts, checks that the file is right, and puts it in place. The game updates itself; the launcher waits for it.
- Plays the game: PLAY starts it, and the launcher waits in the taskbar until you are done.
- Cloud save: keeps your progress and your addons' settings in your own cloud folder (Google Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox or any synced folder), so you can play on any PC. It downloads before you play and uploads after, even after a crash, and keeps backups.
- Addons: switch addons on and off, install approved ones with one click from Download More, keep them up to date and uninstall them. Advanced settings add profiles that keep their own game settings.
- Its own addons: AZS Stats (records every run), AZS Handbook (this Guide inside the game) and AZS Minor Mods (small changes you switch on and off).
- Guide: everything a new player needs, from classes, spells and talents to races, zones, bosses, gear, transmog, controls and tips, with videos.
- Theorycrafting: the Talent Tree Builder and the Gear Planner, to try builds before you play.
- Stats: your play time and every run's numbers, records, Nemesis and co-op.
- Community: players' videos on the home screen, the game's patch notes and the Discord.
- Options: the game's folders, graphics and gameplay settings, and the launcher's own, in 26 languages.

# Look
The launcher layout is inspired by the WoW Retro Launcher by Parquelle (https://github.com/Parquelle/WoWRetroLauncher). All of its artwork is original, drawn for this launcher.
