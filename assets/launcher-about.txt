# The Azeroth Survivors Launcher
A community launcher for Azeroth Survivors, the game made by Aethus. Made by Slimjim. It does four things for you:

# Downloading the game
- One click downloads and installs the whole game, right in the launcher, with a progress bar
- The game comes from one of several hosts, picked at random; if one fails, the next is tried
- Every download is checked against the game's known checksums, so a damaged or wrong file is never installed
- The game updates itself when it starts (it closes and opens again); the launcher notices and keeps going. Reinstalling keeps your addons and settings

# Installing addons automatically
- Download More installs addons with one click and offers an update when a newer copy is out
- The AZS addons (AZS Stats, AZS Handbook, AZS Minor Mods) are built into the launcher and brought up to date by themselves before the game starts
- Switch addons on and off, keep two addon profiles, and uninstall any addon from your AddOns folder

# Helping cloud save on Windows PCs and Linux
- Keeps your save game and your addons' settings in your own cloud folder (Google Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox or any synced folder) and carries them between your machines, installing any addon whose settings arrive without it
- Downloads your progress before the game starts and uploads it when the game closes, even after a crash
- Asks which progress to keep when two machines changed it, and keeps backups before anything is replaced

# New Player Guide, in the launcher and in the game
- Every topic a new player needs: classes, races, zones, bosses, level-ups, gear, talents, controls and tips, with players' build videos
- Each class: its spells with their icons, the talents that change them, and the full talent trees, where you can try a build
- The same guide is inside the game with the AZS Handbook addon (/handbook), always matching the launcher's
- Your stats from every run, in the launcher and in game (AZS Stats)

# Look
The launcher layout is inspired by the WoW Retro Launcher by Parquelle (https://github.com/Parquelle/WoWRetroLauncher). All of its artwork is original, drawn for this launcher.
