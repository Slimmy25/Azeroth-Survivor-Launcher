# v1.7 (2026-10-27)
## Guide
- Class pages no longer end with a NEXT button: hover the class's name in the path at the top to go to another class.
## News and Changelog
- Patch notes for the game's 0.121.0 update: Heroic I to V, marks, star upgrades, 4- and 8-piece set bonuses.

## Game update 0.121.0 (Heroic and gear upgrades)
- Guide: the Run Options topic is now Heroic & Endless: Heroic I to V and how each level opens, the weekly horde
  affixes (Bolstering, Sanguine, Explosive), and the marks that returning endless bosses pay.
- Guide > Gear & the Armory: new Marks, Stars, Set bonuses (4-piece, 8-piece, the PvP sets' crowd control) and Weapon
  glows sections. Boss gear explains bad luck protection, and that a piece you already own now pays marks.
- Guide > Zones & Bosses: bosses aim ahead of where you are running, more so on each Heroic level.
- Guide > Talents, Achievements and Co-op: talent experience rises with the Heroic level, the new Heroic achievements,
  Against the Odds asking for Heroic III, the host choosing the Heroic level, and co-op scaling 20% less per hero.
- Drop chances in the Items topic are marked as normal-difficulty chances.
- AZS Handbook (in game): the same Guide changes.
- AZS Stats (in game): every run now keeps the game's run modifiers, so Heroic runs can be told apart later.

## Set bonuses and stars
- Guide > Items: every class's gear now ends each rarity with its set's 4-piece and 8-piece bonuses, as the game's
  armory words them, for all nine classes (54 sets). Class pages show them under each rarity's pieces too, and the
  spells named in them are highlighted.
- Guide > Classes: a spell's box now also shows "Set bonuses that change it": the sets whose 8-piece bonus changes that
  spell (Fireball: Frostfire 8-piece), in the set's colour. Point at one to read the bonus.
- Gear Planner: pick 0 to 5 stars for each armor piece (the buttons above the list). Stars decide which set bonuses you
  reach (4-piece at 1+ stars, 8-piece at 3+). What a star adds to a piece's own numbers is waiting for clarification
  from the game's developer, so the totals count every piece at 0 stars for now; weapons have no stars yet for the same
  reason. Everything is ready for those numbers.
- Gear Planner: the Set bonuses list now has its own room under the weapons. Four pieces of one set and four of another
  show and count both 4-piece bonuses, and a full set shows its 4-piece next to its 8-piece; a "Reached" line sums up
  which bonuses you have.
- Item cards (Guide hovers and the planner) show the piece's set with its 4-piece and 8-piece bonuses.
- AZS Handbook (in game): the same star buttons, totals, Set bonuses list, set lines on class pages and "Set bonuses
  that change it" on spells.
- Guide: "Mr. Smite" and "High Priest Venoxis" (and every other boss's name) no longer link to Smite or the Priest
  class; in the in-game Handbook, Smite in Mr. Smite is no longer highlighted as the spell.
- Guide > Gear & the Armory: the 4-piece bonuses, mixing two sets, and which PvP sets are built around crowd control
  (the warrior's is built around Mortal Strike and the shaman's around Earth Shock).

## AZS Stats: spell focus
- At the end of a run, AZS Stats says in chat which spells the run leaned on: each spell's share of the ranks you put
  into your spells ("Spell focus (12 ranks): Fireball 42% · Frost Nova 25% ...").
- History (in game and in the launcher) lists each run's spells with their rank and that share, the biggest first.
- Abilities (in game and in the launcher): each spell also shows its share of every rank you have put into spells, so
  you can see which spell you favour over time.

# v1.5 (2026-10-27)
## News and Changelog
- Patch notes for the game's 0.120.5 update: co-op works on networks that blocked the co-op server, and an unreachable
  server is reported as such instead of "the host left".

## Gear Planner and Items
- Every Priest piece now has its bonuses: the Common (Acolyte's), Uncommon (Devout), Rare (Prophecy) and Epic
  (Transcendence) sets and their staves join Legendary and PvP. All 54 Priest pieces count in the Gear Planner's
  totals and in the spell and talent hover numbers; none is marked * any more.
- Warrior, Paladin and Hunter gear now has its bonuses too, read from the game's item cards: every piece of every
  rarity, including the Legendary weapons Sulfuras, Ashbringer and Rhok'delar (each also makes your spells strike 1
  more target). No piece of these three classes shows * any more.
- Rogue, Shaman, Mage, Warlock and Druid gear has its bonuses too, so every class's gear is known now. Every piece of
  every class is known now, so nothing shows * any more. The same Atiesh has its own numbers for each class: the
  Mage's gives 46% damage, 2.4% crit and 1 more target.
- Rogues and shamans get their **Off Hand** in the Gear Planner: the rogue's second dagger and the shaman's shield,
  each with its own bonuses.
- Each class page (Guide > Gameplay > Classes) now ends with its **Gear**: every piece that class can wear, rarity by
  rarity. Point at a piece to read its card; click it to put it on in the Gear Planner.
- On a class page, **TALENT TREES** now sits at the top right, on the same line as CLASSES. The paragraph that
  explained the talent trees further down is gone.
- Under a spell, the line of other spells that name it now reads "Spells that change it", like "Talents that change
  it" above it.
- A hunter's bow or gun goes in the **Ranged** slot of the Gear Planner, as in the game, instead of Main Hand.

## AZS Handbook (in game)
- The in-game Handbook now has everything the launcher's Guide has: each class page ends with that class's **gear**
  (point at a piece for its card, click it to try it on), a **Talent trees** button at the top of each class page, and
  under each spell the **Spells that change it** as well as the talents.
- New in the Handbook: the **Talent Tree Builder** (pick a class; left-click adds a point, right-click takes one away,
  with the same rules as the launcher: up to 51 points, a row opens for every 5 points above it) and the **Gear
  Planner** (pick a class, then a slot and a piece: gear and jewelry, with the totals the launcher shows). Both are on
  the Handbook's first page and link to each other.
- The launcher writes the Handbook's data from its own guide, talents and gear, so the two always match.

## Addons
- An addon that comes as more than one folder is one row in My Addons and switches on and off as one: Survivor UI with
  Survivor Plates, BugSack with !BugGrabber. Uninstalling it removes all of its folders. Rows are sorted by the
  addon's own name.
- My Addons shows each addon's name with its author ("Survivor UI By morderous"); for an addon not from Download More,
  its own title and author.
- The OTHER ADDONS heading is gone for now: addons not from Download More are listed under AZS ADDONS.

## First-time setup
- First-time setup now happens inside the launcher's section window, not in a box over a darkened launcher. It asks for
  your **language first**, then the usual questions. No video plays while it runs; Community comes back when it is done.

## Everything else
- Hovering a step of the path at the top of a window opens its list of the other places at that level; a click on the
  step still goes there.
- The Gear Planner no longer has a TALENTS button (the path above it leads there), so the sheet starts higher.
- Button labels that don't fit their button get smaller letters instead of running over the edges, and so does the
  "This week / Time played" card on the home screen.
- Options > LAUNCHER no longer shows play time: it is on the home screen.
- Credits: the emblem beside the title (the cat heroes) is Slimjim's own art; the "AS" logo the launcher uses as its icon
  is Aethus's.

# v1.3 (2026-10-27)
## Guide
- Every link in the Guide now goes somewhere. A spell named in another spell's text takes you to that spell, even on
  the same page: the page scrolls to it and its box lights up gold for a moment. A talent's name opens its class's
  talent trees, and so do the small talent icons under a spell ("Talents that change it").
- The question-mark mouse pointer is gone from spells, talents and links.
- Under each spell on a class page, below "Talents that change it", a new line "Spells that affect this" shows the
  icons of the class's other spells whose text names that spell word for word (for example, Aimed Shot under Hunter's
  Mark). Hover one to read it, click it to go to that spell. A longer name that only contains it ("Greater Heal" for
  "Heal") doesn't count.

## Navigation
- The last step of the path at the top of a window can now be clicked too: it opens the same list as its arrow, so you
  can switch to another page, class, tab or category from where you are. Steps that had no list now have one: Options
  shows its tab (Options › Cloud Save) with the other tabs, Options › Launcher › Language lists the languages, Stats ›
  a class lists the other classes, and a class's Talents lists the other classes' talents.

## Cloud Save
- The Cloud Save page now sits inside Options › CLOUD SAVE, with no second button to open it: the cloud state, CHECK
  STATUS, DOWNLOAD, UPLOAD, EARLIER DOWNLOADS and the log are all on that tab.

## Speed
- The whole launcher draws much faster:
  - Pictures (the background, buttons, icons, thumbnails) are prepared once at the size they are shown, not resized
    every time the window redraws.
  - The home screen's background, title and headings are drawn once and reused.
  - The Guide's text is laid out once, so scrolling a long page is smooth.
  - The "This week" card reads AZS Stats' file every few seconds, not on every redraw.
- Questions and the first-time setup answer at once: they wait for your click instead of checking for it every
  10 milliseconds. A new setup page appears in one go, and the darkened launcher behind them no longer redraws itself
  whole.

## First-time setup
- A launcher whose own settings folder ("Azeroth Survivor Launcher", next to it) is missing no longer starts the
  first-time setup when the game is right there and has been played (its save or its config folder exists). It
  carries on with them and says so above PLAY. Every setting is still in Options.

# v1.1 (2026-10-27)
## News and Changelog
- Patch notes for the game's 0.119.1, 0.120.0, 0.120.1, 0.120.2 and 0.120.3 updates: co-op runs that survive a
  dropped connection, joining a new party after the last one ended, fewer co-op disconnects, any race with any class
  (Rewards > Races), and boss kills counting for the whole party's achievements.

## Cloud Save
- Cloud Save no longer copies the AddOns folder itself, only your addons' settings and your save. Before, an older copy
  of an addon from another PC could replace one you had just updated.
- When settings arrive from the cloud for an addon in Download More that isn't installed on this PC, the launcher
  installs it, at PLAY or after DOWNLOAD in Cloud Save. Addons switched off stay off.

## Community
- Hovering the Discord symbol on the News line now shows where it goes: https://discord.gg/azerothsurvivors
- MORE VIDEOS: a video you have watched for more than 45 seconds now has a "Watched!" tag over its picture (only the
  time the video really plays counts, not loading or pausing). The tag is remembered between launches.
- MORE VIDEOS: the featured videos stay at the top in their own order; every other video is shuffled into a new random
  order each time you open the page.
- New featured video in the front page's rotation and at the top of MORE VIDEOS: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AxcvhgPfYHU

## The Guide
- New topic **Items** (Gameplay): every class's gear in every rarity (Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, PvP, Legendary)
  with the boss and zone that drop each piece and the chance, and every ring, trinket and amulet with what it does
  and its numbers, read from the game itself. Item names are links: point at one for its card, click it for its page.
- New **Gear Planner** (Theorycrafting): a character sheet laid out like the game's (C). Pick a class, click a slot to
  put in a piece of its gear, an amulet, two rings and two trinkets, and see what they add up to: Health, Damage,
  Haste, Crit, Crit Dmg, Armor, Area, Targets, Echo, Speed, Regen and Pickup, and the Run Stats panel beside it.
  Right-click a slot to empty it. For now the bonuses of the Priest's Legendary and PvP gear are in; the other
  pieces are listed and marked * until their bonuses are added.
- Races: how to open any race to any class on the Rewards tab (new in the game's 0.120.0).
- Achievements & Rewards: the Races reward, and co-op achievements counting for the whole party.
- Talent Tree Builder: leaving a class's talent trees now clears the points you put in, so every visit starts from an
  empty build (before, the last build came back).

## Addons
- New in Download More > AZS ADDONS: Survivor UI by morderous (with Survivor Plates). The launcher always takes the
  addon zip from its newest release (SurvivorUI-v….zip), never the Survivor UI installer next to it.
- MinimapButtonBag: clicking the bag now opens its bar of buttons. It closed itself a moment after opening, and a
  minimap button the game shows by itself could make the bag think the bar was already open. The bar now stays until
  your pointer has been away from it for 3 seconds (change it in its options, right-click the bag). New: /mbb open
  and /mbb close.
- MinimapButtonBag's bag button now sits on the right of the minimap, no longer under the game's damage meter button.
- Addons that can't be on together are now marked as a conflict. The first pair is Survivor UI and AZS Minor Mods
  (both set raid marks on bosses and party members). In My Addons a red bracket joins the two, with CONFLICT down it.
  Switching one on switches the other off. When both end up on any other way (an install, a profile, TOGGLE ALL, or
  by hand), Survivor UI is kept and AZS Minor Mods goes off; PLAY checks this too.
- AZS Minor Mods, chat: one setting now, "No level-up, stat bonus or new spell lines in chat". It hides
  "Congratulations, you have reached level 12!", "You have gained 3% damage, 12 maximum health…" and "You have
  learned a new spell: Cleave (Level 1)", in every language the game has. "Remove bloat from chat" is folded into it.
- AZS Minor Mods: the separate Say & Party box is gone. Say and Party chat stay in the main chat window as usual.
- AZS Minor Mods, new setting: "Plain action bar". The gryphons and the bar's art go, leaving just the six spell
  squares (the racial button and the character and talent buttons are hidden too). Untick it and the bar comes back
  as it was; the character and talent panels still open with their keys (C and N).

## Playing
- When the game updates itself (it closes and opens again), the launcher now follows it: it waits for the game to come
  back (up to 90 seconds when the game's files changed, 10 seconds after a normal close) and treats it as the same
  session. Cloud Save uploads only once the game stays closed, and the status line says "The game updated itself and
  is opening again..." meanwhile.
- The launcher no longer has any game-update code of its own: the game handles its updates itself. The unused update
  checker, its update dialog and its update-file tools are gone, and the UPDATE button is only for the launcher.

## Other
- GitHub now shows the launcher's license as MIT. The note that the game's name, logo and artwork are not covered
  moved from LICENSE to the README.

# v1.0 (2026-10-26)
## Getting the game
- Nothing downloads on its own any more. First-time setup only asks questions, and **INSTALL** asks whether the game
  is already on your PC: point to its folder, or let the launcher download it.
- If all three download hosts fail, the game's own download page is tried as a last resort.
- **Install from a file** is gone: choosing a folder that holds the game does the same. **Import a save** moved into
  Options > FOLDERS.
- Every question and message now opens inside the launcher, and the rest of the window waits until you answer it.
- All messages show in one place, the status line above PLAY.

## The Guide (was the New Player Guide)
- Three groups, each with its topics as tabs: **New Player**, **Gameplay** and **Theorycrafting**. The GUIDE button
  always opens the main page, with every topic as a card.
- New **Talent Tree Builder**: pick a class and plan a build on its three trees. As in the game, each row opens once
  its own tree has 5 points for every row above it; up to 51 points, kept per class.
- New topic **Addons**, and new pages on enraged bosses, the run clock, the damage meter's three views, guardian lairs
  and which parts of the game's screen you can hide.

## Addons
- Every addon is optional and off unless you choose it. Setup asks about AZS Stats on its own.
- Download More shows small blocks, four to a row, with the categories as tabs and a full-width button on each block.
- Your installed addons are grouped by category.
- New **WoW 1.12 addons**, converted to work in this game: **MinimapButtonBag** (every minimap button in one bag) and
  **BugSack** with **!BugGrabber** (every Lua error in one window).
- **Azerloot** is back in Download More, always its newest version.
- Uninstalling with X no longer fails with "access is denied" (a problem in OneDrive folders).
- **AZS Transparency** was a test and is gone; the launcher removes it from your AddOns folder.

## AZS Minor Mods
- A minimap button and a settings window with every option on or off.
- **Move the UI**: unlock, drag the player, target and party frames, minimap, buffs, cast bar, action bars and the
  Say & Party box, then lock it again. Positions are kept.
- **Hide the target frame.**
- No more level-up, stat bonus and "new spell (Level 1)" lines in chat, in every language the game has.
- **Remove bloat from chat**: Say and Party only in their own box.
- **Boss markers**: Skull, Cross and Moon on the bosses, in the order they come. In co-op a friend who falls gets a
  blinking Square.

## AZS Stats and the STATS page
- New: level-up saves (a level-up at 15% health or less), legendaries collected and most in one run, every co-op death
  and what caused it, and the highest DPS per zone, alone and in co-op.

  you while you pick a card.
- New **Armory** tab: each class's hero with their gear and numbers, outside the game.
- The Survivor card only shows on Overview, so the other tabs start with their content.
- Brought in line with the game's addon API: boss kills, DPS and your class are read the way the game provides them,
  and the tracker only shows during a run.

## Community and videos
- Featured videos on the home screen rotate between this week's headliners. **MORE VIDEOS** lists every video.
- YouTube videos play inside the launcher with only the video showing, and fullscreen fills your whole screen.

## Everything else
- Patch notes for the game's 0.117 to 0.119 updates.
- Options > GAME has the game's settings in groups (Display, Graphics, Sound, Gameplay), each explained.
- The changelog opens on the patch before the newest one (News already shows the newest).
- Switching tabs and sections no longer flashes empty grey boxes.
- Every new text is translated into all 26 languages.
