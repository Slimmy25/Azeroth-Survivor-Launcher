- Changing a setting in Options keeps the page where you scrolled it
- The News line: the Discord symbol left of "News", CHANGELOG at the end of the line
- YouTube videos play inside the launcher: the player's own window is found even when Edge hands it to another of its processes, so it no longer opens on its own at the top left of the screen
- Community headlines Asmongold's video for two weeks; 12 new build guide videos by Holdherhand and Des
- New AZS addons, built into the launcher (Addons > Download More > AZS ADDONS) and kept up to date by it before the game starts: AZS Handbook (the whole New Player Guide inside the game, /handbook), AZS Minor Mods (the level-up text out of the chat and a Say & Party box above it, /azsmods) and AZS Transparency (a test: which of your spells each item upgrade makes stronger, /transparency). All AZS addons share AZS Stats' look, and each has its own spot by the minimap
- AZS ADDONS in Download More are shown as blocks, two to a row
- The installed addons list has an X beside each addon to uninstall it from the AddOns folder
- About Launcher describes what the launcher does: downloading the game, installing addons, cloud save and the New Player Guide
- Each class page in the Guide has a TALENT TREES button: the class's three trees, where you can try out a build of up to 51 points (click to add a point, right-click to take one back; no talent takes more than its ranks). Your test build is kept for each class
- Every class page in the Guide shows its spells with their icons, the talents that change each spell (point at one to read it), and the class's three full talent trees as the game draws them: every talent with its icon and ranks, the 10, 20 and 30 point milestones marked, read from the game itself
- Options has tabs: FOLDERS, GENERAL, LANGUAGE and LAUNCHER. UPDATE LAUNCHER and ABOUT LAUNCHER moved into Options > LAUNCHER
- The Discord button is now Discord's own symbol, on the News line at the right edge
- The guide also finds spells written differently ("Shadowbolt", "Shadow Bolts"), and when AZS Stats has seen a spell's tooltip in game, pointing at the spell in the guide shows the game's own text
- INSTALL no longer opens a window: the download and install run in the launcher's status line, with the percentage bar; press INSTALLING to stop the download. Options has INSTALL FROM A FILE for a game you downloaded yourself
- The New Player Guide links its pages together: click a spell, talent, class, race or zone in the text to go to it, and point at it to read what it does (talents, classes, races and zones in the game's own words)
- The section area is exactly as tall as the News column; "This week" and your play time sit together under the Launcher buttons
- Community can no longer be closed; opening another section replaces it
- Options is one scrolling page, and every new text is translated into all 26 languages; long button labels now fit in every language
- DISCORD opens the new invite, discord.gg/azerothsurvivors
- New Community section: it opens on the home screen whenever no other section is open and features one of the players' build videos (credited to its maker), a different one every 10 minutes. A click plays it right inside the section's borders, and so do the Guide's YouTube videos; nothing starts by itself
- "This week" now sits centred at the bottom of the window
- AZS Stats live tracker: PACE shows whether you are ahead of or behind your record run, second by second and minute by minute (a new class DPS record becomes the pace to beat)
- AZS Stats highlights: highest crit, highest normal hit, highest heal and most kills in 1 and 5 seconds, per run, per class and overall; beating your best says so in chat
- AZS Stats minimap button: at the bottom of the minimap (it was under the crossed swords button), above other buttons, click to open the window, drag to move it
- New Build Guides in the GUIDE: players' videos of their builds, each with its title and credit to the uploader; YouTube videos open in your web browser
- Download More has two categories: AZS ADDONS (formerly AS ADDONS) and WOW 1.12 ADDONS; the UI / gameplay / cosmetic filters and the My Addons tab are gone
- Before the game is installed, only Cloud Save and Addons are grayed out (both say why); the Changelog, Guide, Stats, Options and About work as usual
- While the game downloads, its window steps aside and the download bar shows in the status line above INSTALLING, so the Guide and everything else can be used; INSTALLING brings the window back (CANCEL)
- The game download uses the main host or the new backup host at random (50/50); if one fails, the other is tried
- INSTALL now downloads the game inside the launcher with a percentage bar (into a temp folder next to the launcher, deleted afterwards), then installs it
- First-time setup and RUN SETUP AGAIN start by downloading and installing the game; installing again keeps your addons, addon settings and options
- The "Cloud sync successfully downloaded / uploaded" line is gone; only problems are shown there now
- AZS Stats in game: the faces of your Nemesis and of the enemies in your death log, a centred portrait ring, and the minimap button moved to the bottom of the minimap
- About Launcher describes everything the launcher does
- New look: a wide window in the style of the classic WoW launchers, with original art. News shows the newest patch; the sections open inside the launcher on the left instead of in a second window

- Launcher updates and addon downloads now come from a public downloads page, so every player can get them

# v0.3
- Installs a newer launcher by itself when it starts (it waits while the game, an install, a sync or setup is running)

- The addon list no longer shows download addresses
