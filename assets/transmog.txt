Transmog: the looks of Azeroth Survivors, entered by hand from the game's own Armory (0.123.0): Appearances, Tabards
and weapon glows. They add nothing to a hero's numbers. One look per line:
  kind | slot | name | how it is earned | icon (the game's icon name, when known; empty until it is) | item
  item: the real World of Warcraft item the look is (its Wowhead classic item id), when its name differs from the look's
kind: appearance, tabard, glow. An icon is left empty until it is known. A look that is a real item shows its 3D model on mouseover (item-models.txt).
Lines starting with # and everything above the first === line are ignored.
