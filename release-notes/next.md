## What's new

### Gear Planner: stars
- Click a piece on the character sheet to add a star: each click adds one, up to 5, and the next click goes back to 0.
  A small number on the piece shows its stars. The star buttons above the list are gone. Right-click still takes the
  piece off, and a newly picked piece starts at 0 stars.
- Weapons take stars too (all pieces go up to 5).
- Stars decide which set bonuses you reach (4-piece at 1+ stars, 8-piece at 3+). What a star adds to a piece, and what
  it costs, the game does not disclose, so the planner's numbers never count it (no more "waiting for clarification").
- AZS Handbook (in game): the same: click a piece under Slots to add a star, its stars shown as [3].

### Guide
- Gear & the Armory: Marks and Stars rewritten. Marks come from completing Heroic runs and from duplicate pieces (paid
  the moment they drop, even if you leave the run), and belong to the class that earned them. Each Heroic level opens
  the next star (Heroic III also opens the 8-piece bonuses), weapons go to 5 stars too, and an upgraded lower set never
  matches a fully upgraded higher one but leans into the spells you like.
