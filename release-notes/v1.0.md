The first full release of the Azeroth Survivors Launcher.

- **Gets the game for you**: asks if you already have it, otherwise downloads it from the game's hosts (checked against the game's checksums) and keeps the game and the launcher up to date.
- **Addon manager**: turn addons on and off, profiles, uninstall, and Download More with AZS addons and classic addons converted for this game. Every addon is optional.
- **The Guide**: New Player, Gameplay and Theorycrafting, with a search over every page and the same guide in game (AZS Handbook).
- **Theorycrafting**: the Talent Tree Builder, players' build guides, and STATS with your records, best builds and an Armory of your heroes (AZS Stats).
- **Community videos** on the home screen, played inside the launcher.
- Cloud Save, the game's patch notes, the game's own settings explained, 26 languages.

Download `Azeroth Survivors Launcher.exe` below and put it in your game folder (or where you want the game).
