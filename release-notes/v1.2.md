Azeroth Survivors Launcher v1.2.

Launchers already installed update themselves. New players: download `Azeroth Survivors Launcher.exe` below and put it in your game folder.

## In short
More room everywhere: a bigger window, a path at the top instead of rows of buttons, and Cloud Save moved into Options. Download More gets UI, Gameplay and Misc tabs, there's a new THEORYCRAFTING button, and the Gear Planner and Talent Tree Builder now work together.

## All the changes

### News and Changelog
- Patch notes for the game's 0.120.4 update: co-op parties of three or more play smoothly again.

### More room in every section
- The section window is bigger: it now reaches from under the game's name down to the bottom bar.
- The back arrow moved into the title bar, followed by a trail of where you are, for example
  "Guide › Theorycrafting › Talent Tree Builder › Priest". Click any step to go back to it. The rows of tabs you have
  already chosen from (the Guide's groups and topics) are no longer shown inside the page, so the content gets
  their room; go back with the trail to pick something else. The Guide's search box is on its main page.
- The Guide's PREVIOUS / NEXT row and page dots are gone: the page uses that room.
- Talent Tree Builder: the text explaining talents moved behind a **?** button beside RESET.
- Stats: the UPDATE STATS ADDON and REFRESH buttons are gone (the launcher updates AZS Stats before every game);
  a class's page is reached and left through the trail (Stats › Mage).
- Addons, Options, Changelog, About, Cloud Save and Community start right at the top, without the empty row the
  back arrow used to take.

### Addons
- Download More has new tabs: **UI**, **Gameplay** and **Misc** for the AZS addons, and WoW 1.12 Addons.
- UI is pick-one: Survivor UI, SurvivorsDragonUi and Keijin UI can't be on together. Switching one on now asks
  whether to switch the other off (Yes switches it off, No leaves things as they were). The same goes for Survivor UI
  and AZS Minor Mods. When two are found on any other way, Survivor UI is the one kept.
- My Addons lists every addon that is not a WoW 1.12 one under one heading, AZS ADDONS.
- My Addons: DOWNLOAD MORE moved into the right-hand column, so the buttons no longer overlap.
- My Addons: addons that can't be on together and are both on are shown first, under CONFLICT: PICK ONE, joined by
  a red bracket. Untick the one you don't want and both go back to their place in A to Z order.
- Uninstalling an addon (its X) no longer flashes the launcher's background and a black screen before asking: the
  question now opens straight over the launcher as it is. The same goes for every question inside the launcher.
- The README's list of download hosts names who runs each one: a Mirror By Community Member (Leo), a Mirror By
  Community Member (Meexa) and a Mirror By Discord Mod (Fotze).

### Launcher buttons and Options
- New **THEORYCRAFTING** button beside GUIDE: it opens the Guide's Theorycrafting page (Talent Tree Builder, Gear
  Planner, Build Guides). Each group has its own page in the trail too: click "Gameplay" in Guide › Gameplay › Classes.
- The Talent Tree Builder and the Gear Planner come up in the Guide's search, and their names are links anywhere in
  the Guide.
- The Stats buttons are disabled while AZS Stats is not installed and switched on; hovering says "Disabled when AZS
  Stats is not active". The INSTALL STATS ADDON button on the Stats page is gone.
- Cloud Save moved into Options: a new **CLOUD SAVE** tab holds cloud saving on or off, the sync folder (moved from
  FOLDERS) and OPEN CLOUD SAVE › for syncing now, the log and earlier downloads.
- The game's settings are now two tabs: **GRAPHICS** (display and graphics) and **GAMEPLAY** (gameplay and sound).
- GENERAL is gone: its settings and the play time are on the **LAUNCHER** tab, and so is the language: LANGUAGE › opens
  the list of languages (Options › Launcher › Language).

### The Guide
- At the end of a page, **NEXT** takes you to the next page of the topic, then to the next topic of the same group
  (Getting Started, Controls, Your First Run, ...), so the New Player guide reads in order.

### Theorycrafting
- The Talent Tree Builder and the Gear Planner both start with one question in the middle of the window: "What class
  do you want to build for?". The class rows are gone; change class from the trail (Gear Planner › Priest).
- The two tools are linked and share the class: GEAR PLANNER › on the talent trees, TALENTS › in the Gear Planner.
- Gear Planner: the Run Stats panel is replaced by a selector. Click a slot and the panel lists what it can hold,
  each with its picture at the size of its name (rings, trinkets and amulets always; gear once AZS Stats has seen
  it worn), in its rarity's color. Click one to put it in, or Empty.
- With a set in the Gear Planner, the hover texts of spells and talents show their numbers with it: damage with the
  set's damage bonus, cooldowns and "every N sec" with its cooldown bonus, "within N yd" with its area bonus, and a
  line saying which bonuses are counted. It is an estimate (bonuses added together), and the set is forgotten when
  the launcher closes.
- The path at the top of each window (like Guide › Theorycrafting › Talent Tree Builder) now has a small arrow on every step after the first. Click it to jump to another category at that level: another guide group, another topic or tool, another page, another class, another Options tab or another Download More category.
- The "What class do you want to build for?" question now shows over the launcher's background picture, not a black screen.
- Big streamer videos (Asmongold's and five more) are always first in MORE VIDEOS, and the front page features them until November 9. Every other video counts as a Content Creator video.
