## In short

The Guide is organized into New Player and Gameplay, and its final pages can be saved to ten numbered Quick Access favorites.

## Guide and Quick Access
- Split the Guide into New Player and Gameplay sections, with Theorycrafting under Gameplay and links preserved between topics.
- Put Items and Transmog in Gameplay as separate categories; Gear & the Armory stays in New Player.
- Hover a final Guide page and click its heart to add it to the next Quick Access slot.
- Added ten numbered Quick Access slots. Click a favorite to open it, hold and drag to reorder, or right-click to remove it.
