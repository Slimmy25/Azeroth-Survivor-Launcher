## In short

The Guide is organized into New Player and Gameplay, its final pages can be saved to ten numbered Quick Access favorites, and the Gear Planner explains each set bonus before you choose it.

## Guide and Quick Access
- Split the Guide into New Player and Gameplay sections, with Theorycrafting under Gameplay and links preserved between topics.
- Put Items and Transmog in Gameplay as separate categories; Gear & the Armory stays in New Player.
- Hover a final Guide page and click its heart to add it to the next Quick Access slot.
- Added ten numbered Quick Access slots. Click a favorite to open it, hold and drag to reorder, or right-click to remove it.
- In the Gear Planner's Tier Bonus picker, hover over a 4-piece or 8-piece choice to read its bonus and the armor-star requirement before applying it.
- The Quick Access heart stays centered on the hovered path name, and the Lost? note remains anchored to its button with a short explanation and example.
- Talent Tree Builder favorites use the Warcraft Wiki talent button; selecting a saved build from the drop-down loads it, and the action buttons stay centered and evenly spaced as the panel resizes.
