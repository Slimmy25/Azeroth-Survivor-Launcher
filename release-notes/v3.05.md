# Azeroth Survivors Launcher v3.05

## First-time setup

- The setup marker beside the launcher now always requires setup, regardless of its contents. It moves into that install's state folder only after setup succeeds.
- Setup branches for players moving from the website and players who already have the game. New players start with cloud saving off, and existing players can choose their folders.
- The game can download while you browse Download More. The launcher remains open until the download and installation finish unless you choose to cancel and close.

## Closing and hover cards

- Closing the launcher exits it instead of hiding it in the tray or minimizing it when the game starts.
- During a game download or installation, the close choice appears inside the secondary menu.
- Hover cards stay within the launcher window and hide when it loses focus or moves.
